# Accessibility and UX Standards

## Core Rules

- Use semantic HTML
- Keep keyboard focus visible
- Use links for navigation and buttons for actions
- Keep text readable on mobile
- Avoid horizontal overflow

## Current Pricing UX Expectations

- Homepage uses audience-first routing — quiz is secondary, not the primary CTA
- Homepage pricing preview should stay concise
- Membership pricing belongs on `/membership`
- Pricing tables or cards should remain mobile-friendly
- Disclaimers should stay visible near pricing content

## Navigation Expectations

- Do not change the main navigation strategy without approval
- Do not add brokers to main navigation
- Footer or campaign placement for brokers is acceptable

## Content Safety UX

- Do not present guaranteed savings as fact
- Do not imply Direct Primary Care replaces insurance
- Keep “subject to change” language where pricing is shown
